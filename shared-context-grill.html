Discovery sketch · provisional choices

Shared memory keeps the claim and its evidence together.

A crew message can become shared context. A source, a time range, and an explicit uncertainty help a different user understand what the message proves.

All dates and crew names are illustrative.
Timezone and end-time rules are unresolved.

  1. 1

    Original message

    “FYI, WELL-0001 is under emergency maintenance from now until 20 Oct.”

    Source: crew chat / message 042
    Author: Asha · 17 Oct 2026, 09:00

    Keep the source text and the author so another user can inspect the claim.

  3. 3

    Validate and compare

    • Resolve the well ID and date meaning.
    • Check the author and source permissions.
    • Compare existing claims for this time range.

    A confirmation policy and conflict policy are still open design questions.

  4. 4

    Durable shared store

    Retain the message, the assertion, the time range, and the source link.

    Retrieve by well, time, and user access.

    A chat summary alone can lose dates, sources, and conflicting claims.

    Answer includes source 042, its reported time, and the unresolved end time.

Observed production

Weekly inspection evidence at its observation time.

Example: a temperature-based production inference needs the applicable rule and measurements.

Reported maintenance

A crew report says emergency maintenance is planned through 20 Oct.

A report has a source. It is not a fresh sensor reading.

Actual current production

Unknown from this maintenance message.

Maintenance duration does not establish inspection-derived production downtime.

Decision to test: preserve separate claims and evidence, then resolve them during retrieval. A planned end date can pass without a confirmed restart.
